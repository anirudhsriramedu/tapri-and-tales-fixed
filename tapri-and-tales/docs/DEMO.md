# Judge verification path (3 minutes)
1. From Home choose Judge Mode. In Buy Stock, buy a starter pack, watch cash and inventory change. Visit Menu & Pricing and adjust a selling price.
2. Go to Customer Rush, click Open Stall and Quick Day, then Daily Report. Explain Revenue − Consumed Ingredient COGS − Operating Expenses = Net Profit. Purchases reduce cash, not profit.
3. Advance to next day to trigger the 2× Milk Supply Shock. Show the three decision projections using the same deterministic day engine. Choose one strategy and use Quick Day again.
